WHY WE’RE DOING THIS

More birds.
Less discarded food.

Trichomonosis is killing finches at an alarming rate. UK greenfinch numbers have fallen by around 60% since 2005, with trichomonosis driving the decline. Source: Yorkshire Dales Wildlife Rescue ↗
The disease spreads through contaminated food. The RSPB advises against flat feeders and bird tables. Our feeder is designed to collect falling debris.
